Security & Settings

Manage your profile, display preferences, and sign-in security.

Profile

Your email address is read-only. Choose a display name up to 120 characters.

Preferences

Display currency changes reporting conversions only; saved transaction amounts are not changed.

Change password

Use at least 12 characters. After changing it, you’ll need to sign in again on this device and all previously issued tokens will stop working.

Session management

WealthFlow does not track individual devices or show an active-session list. Logging out or changing your password revokes previously issued sign-in tokens.

Privacy & account data

WealthFlow stores your email address, display name, password hash, preferences, transactions, budgets, recurring transactions, saving goals, asset and liability estimates, historical net-worth snapshots, and support requests you submit. Passwords are not stored as plain text. Your financial records are scoped to your account.

Download transaction data (Excel)

Help center

Getting started

Create an account or sign in, choose a display currency, then add your first transaction from the Transactions section.

Transactions

Add income or expenses, filter the ledger, use the pencil to edit, or the trash icon to delete a transaction.

Charts & insights

The dashboard summarizes recorded income and expenses. Charts, projected spending, and insights update from your transactions.

Goals & budgets

Saving goals track goal-specific savings separately from transactions. Budgets set category limits for a selected month and year.

Import & export

Import .xlsx or UTF-8 .csv after reviewing the preview. CSV and Excel exports download your own transaction records.

Account & security

Update your display name and preferences in Settings. Password changes require your current password and sign you out.

Recurring transactions

Schedule recurring rules with a due date and frequency. Removing a rule deactivates it and preserves transaction history.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a transaction?

Use the Add transaction form next to the ledger. Enter date, type, amount, and category, then submit.

How do I correct an incorrect transaction?

Select the pencil icon on its ledger row, change the fields, and save. Deletion is available from the trash icon.

How do I import or export transactions?

Use Import Transactions to preview an .xlsx or .csv file before confirmation. Use the CSV or Excel actions in the ledger to export the signed-in user’s transactions.

How do I change my display currency?

Change Display currency in Settings or the dashboard header. Existing transaction amounts stay in their original currencies; dashboard values are converted for display.

How do I change my password?

In Settings, enter your current password and a matching new password of at least 12 characters. You’ll be signed out and must log in again.

What if I cannot log in?

Check that the email address is correct and try your password again. There is no password-reset email configured in this installation.

How can I protect my financial information?

Use a unique password, sign out on shared devices, and use HTTPS for remote deployments. Never share your password or downloaded financial reports.

Can I delete my account?

Self-service deletion is not available. Contact your application administrator; submitting the support form below only saves a local request and does not send an email.

Troubleshooting

  • If a dashboard section appears blank, refresh the page and sign in again.
  • If an import is rejected, check the required columns, supported currencies, row errors, and file size limit.
  • If your password was changed elsewhere, sign in again; older sessions are revoked.
  • If charts do not load, check your connection to the chart library CDN and refresh the dashboard.
